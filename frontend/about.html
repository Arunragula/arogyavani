How We Protect Your Personal Identity

When you upload a medical PDF or photo directly to general chatbots, your full Name, Phone Number, Passport/UHID, Address, and Doctor details are exposed. CuraVault AI solves this with a 4-stage pipeline:

Step 1: Local Table Extraction

We extract only the text and table alignment locally without sending your raw high-res document pixels to third parties.

Step 2: Personal Identifier Scrubbing

Patient Names, Doctor Names, Phone/Fax Numbers, Emails, Clinics, and IDs are automatically masked.

Step 3: Anonymous Clinical Context

Only anonymous Age & Gender (e.g., 23 Years, Male) are kept so the AI can match accurate healthy human reference ranges.

Step 4: Locked Multi-Language Vault

Your report is fingerprinted so switching between English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, or Arabic always gives 100% consistent medical facts.
